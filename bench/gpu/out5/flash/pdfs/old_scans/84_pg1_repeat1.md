A note just now impresses me that our 4 companies of Engineers per Vt. Goodspeed name started at the assent of this city some ten or more persons for these supplies from [the] the Boston are compelled to sleep on bare loose floors with a simple blanket and are taking some colds and coughing [frightfully] fearfully. — my injured points no way of relief but to buy so many tons of ticks to be filled with hay to raise them from the draft a little. They are far more exposed than they could be on the ground under a good tent. — I almost say your ladies when so many if you can work together and accomplish so much — like my pen buters are so single handeded. The future often looks dark to me & and it seems sometimes that the Sonles of Years are almost withdrawn from our present & distracted country and yet

there is everything to be grateful for found by no means the least is this strongly wild venture

But I must desist and cease perdu for my (perhaps unparadensibly) long letter, for if you have followed me thus far, and especially at comparatively rapid a rate as I have written you must be weary. I did not intend to say so much but let my interest be my apology. And with one more final word in answer to your rational question I have done, Ladies remember, that the call for your organized efforts in behalf of our army, was not from any Commission or Committee but from Abraham Lincoln & Simon Cameron and when they no longer need your libles they will tell you
