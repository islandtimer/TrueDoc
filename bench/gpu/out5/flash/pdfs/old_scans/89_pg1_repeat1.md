Commonwealth of Massachusetts.
Executive Department.
Boston, March 24th 1862

Miss Clara H. Barton
North Oxford, Mass.

Dear Miss Barton,
I beg to express to Miss Barton, my cordial sympathy with you most worthy sentiments & wishes; that I have any power to promote your design & are your friends I will freely use it. Whenever you may be ready with Genl. Burnides' division I will cheerfully give you a cable introduction, with my hearty
