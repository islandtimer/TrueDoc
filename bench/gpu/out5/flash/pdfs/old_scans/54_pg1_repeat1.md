It was such a night as this: There was not a breath of air to ruffle the smooth surface of the calm Adiriac: naught was heard save the distant tinkling of the solitary sheep bell, & the low backing of the honest watchdog. He stood beside a tomb—a deep high rent his bowers & even anon as he cut his tearful languid eye to the pale, haste moon, [any] heaven he exclaimed: oh God! what a night—for catching coons!
