Bangor. Pa. May 22nd 1914.
Col. Rosevelt Comrade & Friend
Dear Sir
I am one of Gen D.E. Sickles old Regt & Brigade I Served through all of the civil War. During the 5 years was with Gen Sickles when He Lost His Leg. Then He Left us. I Regret your Absence from this country During your trip to S.A. I saw Mr Pinchott Here & Had a Little talk with Him about you & the Political Situation of the country I am with you for the Election of Gifford Pinchott as U.S. Senator. One I saw Here must Be Repealed as we are Deprived of Voting for Whosever We Desire at the Primaries. if you come to Easton or Bangor Pa I would Like to See you Address 25 Market St Bangor
Yours very Resp. Caleb Aber
