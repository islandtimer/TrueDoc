On yesterday to his surprise and to the surprise of all his neighbors, he received an order from Gen Lewis Merrell requiring him to leave the State within fifteen days and take up his residence somewhere in the extreme North. Mr. Fisher is an old man, has the respect of those know him, and is a devoted and useful minister. He will have a feeble wife and young daughters behind him comparatively helpless. I should not suffer my sympathies for him or any other if guilty to interfere with his punishment. But I think here is a grievous wrong to be inflicted upon gray hairs undeserving it, and that two in the name of the Constitution

and country that we love and venerate. I know that Military Law is harsh and unfeeling. Yet I do not impute in any degree the wisdom or good intentions of those in power.
