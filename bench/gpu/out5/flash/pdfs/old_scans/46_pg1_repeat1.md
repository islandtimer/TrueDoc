Hon: J Holt

St. Louis January 10. '62

Dear Sir
My rent due 19th Decr has not been paid, and as the sum of vital importance, not inconvenient interest inform me whether have any claims agst same may be paid that he would pay out of the money, as soon as possible. After my loss & sacrifice to serve a worthy income for my family during war times, it will be exceedingly hard to lose this debt. My deep anxiety in regard to this, avail my last resource, will excuse this trouble.
I open my office as you will perceive from word can.

Very truly &c
A. Burwell
