St Louis Jan 13th 1862

Dr Sir.

A friend informs me that a few weeks since he addressed a letter to me at Louisville, Ky, supposing me to be there. If received, it has probably been advertised. Please forward it to me here & oblige very respectfully

your obt. servt
W Holt

Dr L. Speed
Postmaster

Louisville

I amed Picketry you addressed & forwarded a number of letters to you at St. Louis.

William L. Kelly

Assist on

Janry 15/62
Louisville Ky 3
