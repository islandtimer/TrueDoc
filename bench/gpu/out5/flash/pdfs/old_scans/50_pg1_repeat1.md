Keeping Sabbath

A plain man desires to comply with what he believes to be the will of God, but conscious of his own fault, he revolts from enforcing others to conform to his own opinion. Such compulsion, I presume what shape it may be the expense of persecution - God has given man no authority to coerce unobedience to his precepts.

Religion is a thing between a man & his maker - surely it be the voluntary offering of the best, it is but a vain cold repetition of words, which cannot be acceptable to God - If a man does not believe that religion exacts it of him as a duty to obtain from labor all the Sabbath, he will submit with reluctance & in this submission there will be no pity on his part nor will it advance the pity of others.

There can be no more justice or tolerance in this law than there would be in enforcing favor of Catholics, upon the prohibited, an abstinence from meat during Lent, or in enforcing the [Nehemian] Christians to worship Mohamet

1st There is no one precept in the new Testament commanding us to keep a Sabbath - If we are bound to keep one, it is in consequence of the Mosaic law

2. The language of the 4th commandment is, "The seventh day is the Sabbath of the Lord" But the Christians keep the first day, not the seventh

3. There is not a single word either in the old or new Testament, nor even an allusion relating to the substitution of the first day for the seventh - The subject is not mentioned in any of the discourse of Christ nor in any of the epistles of his apostles
