5/26/14

Dear Mr. President,

Confirming my telegram,
Mrs. Butler and I will be delighted to have
you as our guest while you are in Washington
next week, on the occasion of your lecture
before the National Geographical Society.

Trusting that we will have the
pleasure of entertaining you, and that your
health is being rapidly restored, I am,

Very sincerely yours,

Marion Butler
