Duncant Logick

1. what in Logick A. the art of reasoning & communicating knowledge 2. into how many parts is it divided A. into four 3. of what do they treat? A. the first treat of Simple apprehension 2 of judgement. 3d of reasoning & 4th of method. 4. what is Simple apprehension? A. it is the attention of the mind to the impression made upon it by surrounding objects 5. what is consciousness? A. It is the attention of the mind to its own operations 6. what are the sources from which all our [knowledge] origin of ideas 7. Sensation & reflection A. desire? 7. what ideas do we get from sensation? A. our idea of heat, cold, colour &c. 8. what ideas do we get from reflection? A. our idea of thinking, willing, believing &c. 9. how are our ideas divided? A. into Simple & complex 10. what is a Simple Idea? A. it is an original impression existing [under] in the mind under one uniform appearance, without variety or composition 11. give an instance of a simple idea of sensation & also of reflection A. [est] the idea we have of colour is a simple idea of sensation & the idea we have of willing is a simple idea of reflection.
