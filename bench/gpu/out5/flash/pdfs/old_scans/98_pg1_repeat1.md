Washington D.C. Feb 22nd '03

To the members of the
Military Comtee U.S.S.

Senators

Nothing less

than a strong conviction of duty owed to one
of the brave defenders of our Nation's honor
could induce me to intrude for a moment upon
the already burdened, and limited term of action
yet remaining to your Honorable Body.

the 21st Massachusetts Regiment of Volunteers
were ordered to charge upon a Battery across an
open field; in the terrible fire which assailed
them, the colors were three times in quick suc-
cession bereft of their support; the third time
they were siezed by Sergeant Thomas Plunkett
of Co. E, and borne over some three hundred
yards of open space, when a shell from the Enemies
Battery in its murderous course killed three
