Private and confidential.

Central College,

Fayette, Mo

Fany 1st 1862

Hon J Holt.

Dear Sir.

Allow me in view of the pleasant relating and recollections of other years and the confidence I have in your high regard for justice right and humanity, to invoke your aid in a matter which I will briefly present. Rev. David Fisher residing in this neighborhood a minister of the M.E. Church, South was some months ago arrested by the Military as were nearly all of our ministers.
