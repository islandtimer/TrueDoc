Ex. 5. $y = \sqrt{x^2 - 1}$ .

Using VI, with $u = x^2 - 1$ ,

$$\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{d}{dx} (x^2 - 1)^{\frac{1}{2}} = \frac{1}{2} (x^2 - 1)^{-\frac{1}{2}} \frac{d}{dx} (x^2 - 1)$$
$$= \frac{1}{2} (x^2 - 1)^{-\frac{1}{2}} (2 x) = \frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2 - 1}}.$$

Ex. 6. $x^2 + xy - y^2 = 1$ .

We can consider $y$ a function of $x$ determined by the equation. Then

$$d (x^2) + d (xy) - d (y^2) = d (1) = 0,$$

that is,

$$2 x dx + x dy + y dx - 2 y dy = 0,$$
$$(2 x + y) dx + (x - 2 y) dy = 0.$$

Consequently,

$$\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{2 x + y}{2 y - x}.$$

Ex. 7. $x = t + \frac{1}{t} , y = t - \frac{1}{t}$ .

In this case

$$dx = dt - \frac{dt}{t^2}, \quad dy = dt + \frac{dt}{t^2}.$$

Consequently,

$$\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{1 + \frac{1}{t^2}}{1 - \frac{1}{t^2}} = \frac{t^2 + 1}{t^2 - 1}.$$
