Divisions of Logick.

1. what is Logick?

A. the art of reasoning & communicating knowledge, the operations of the human mind in acquiring or that science which explains

2. into how many parts is it divided?

A. into four.

3. of what do they treat?

A. the first treats of Simple apprehension. 2d of judgement. 3d of reasoning & 4th of method.

4. what is Simple apprehension?

A. it is the attention of the mind to the impressions made upon it by surrounding objects.

5. what is consciousness?

A. it is the attention of the mind to its own operations.

6. what are the sources from which all our [knowledge] original ideas are derived?

A. Sensation & reflection.

7. what ideas do we get from sensation?

A. our ideas of heat, cold, colour &c.

8. what ideas do we get from reflection?

A. our ideas of thinking, willing, believing &c.

9. how are our ideas divided?

A. into Simple & complex.

10. what is a simple idea?

A. it is an original impression existing [under] in the mind under one uniform appearance, without variety or composition.

11. give an instance of a simple idea of sensation & also of reflection.

A. [cat] the idea we have of colour is a simple idea of sensation & the idea we have of willing is a simple idea of reflection.
