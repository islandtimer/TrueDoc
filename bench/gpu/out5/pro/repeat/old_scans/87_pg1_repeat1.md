I do not think, at the present time, Miss Barton had better undertake to go to Burnside's division to act as a nurse. Alfred Hithcock March 25th 1862.

J. W. Flotey letter & request to send Miss Barton to nurse sick & wounded soldiers is reasonably decided.

Respectfully referred for the information of Miss Barton, J. A. Anderson, Mar. 15/62
