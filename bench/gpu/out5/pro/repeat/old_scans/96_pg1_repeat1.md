Camp near Falmouth, Va Head quarters Genl. Sturges (2nd) Division Dec. 8th 1862. —

Mr J Brown & Co.

Dear friends

Mr Wells returns tomorrow, and I improve the opportunity to send a line by him to you not feeling quite certain if posted matter reaches directly when sent from the Army.

We reached Aquia Creek safely in the time anticipated and to my great joy learned immediately that our old friend Capt (Maj.) Hall (of the 21st) was Q. Master as soon as the Boat was unloaded he came on board and spent the remainder of the evening with me, — we had a home chat I assure you — — remained till the next day, sent a bbl of apples etc up to the Capt's quarters, and proceeded with the remainder of our luggage for which, it is needless to say, ready transportation
