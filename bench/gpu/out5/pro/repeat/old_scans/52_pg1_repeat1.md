Crusades I must write a composition on the crusades showing the design with which they were undertaken, the zeal with which all the Christian princes engaged in them & the good effect it produced in Europe by bringing the military art to perfection, polishing the manners of the people & destroying a great measure the feudal system. This last war one of the greatest blessings it conferred upon the civilized world. — The historical part of these crusades may be found in an abridged form in Lytton's History
