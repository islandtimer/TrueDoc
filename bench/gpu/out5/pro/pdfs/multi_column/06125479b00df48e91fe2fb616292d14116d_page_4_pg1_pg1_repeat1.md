Tell Gov. Bullock to Leave a Wild Legacy

Montana Governor Steve Bullock has his sights set on the White House. Tell him you take the buffalo into the voting booth with you! Ask Gov. Bullock for a new decision with public involvement and comment to protect more year-round habitat for wild buffalo, including the south side of the Madison River on the Custer Gallatin National Forest, where hazing still takes place on cattle-free public land.

He can order the Montana executive branch to start a public process and leave a legacy that this country can be proud of! Phone calls are best because they can't be ignored, but you can back your call up with an email. Call Gov. Bullock at (406) 444-3111 and email governor@mt.gov. Wild is the Way—Roam Free!
