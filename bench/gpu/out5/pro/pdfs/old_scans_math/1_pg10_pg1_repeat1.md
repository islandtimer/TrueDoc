EXAMPLES ON THE INTEGRAL CALCULUS.

CHAPTER I.

ELEMENTARY INTEGRALS TO BE COMMITTED TO MEMORY.

$$(1.) \int \frac{d x}{x} = \log x. (2.) \int \frac{d x}{a+b x^2} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{a b}} \tan^{-1} \left( x \sqrt{\frac{b}{a}} \right), \text{ or } \int \frac{d x}{a^2+x^2} \\ = \frac{1}{a} \tan^{-1} \frac{x}{a}. (3.) \int \frac{d x}{\sqrt{a^2-x^2}} = \sin^{-1} \frac{x}{a}. (4.) \int \frac{-d x}{\sqrt{a^2-x^2}} = \cos^{-1} \frac{x}{a}. (5.) \int \frac{d x}{\sqrt{x^2 \pm a^2}} = \log \frac{(x + \sqrt{x^2 \pm a^2})}{a}. (6.) \int \frac{d x}{\sqrt{2 a x - x^2}} = \text{vers}^{-1} \frac{x}{a}. (7.) \int \frac{d x}{\sqrt{x^2 \pm 2 a x}} = \log (x \pm a + \sqrt{x^2 \pm 2 a x}). (8.) \int \frac{d x}{x \sqrt{x^2 - a^2}} = \frac{1}{a} \sec^{-1} \frac{x}{a}.$$
