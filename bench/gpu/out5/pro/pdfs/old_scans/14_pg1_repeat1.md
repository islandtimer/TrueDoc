West New Brighton S. I. N. Y. 5-22-14

My Dear Col Roosevelt It has added some years to my life to know that you have returned to God's land safe, sane and sound and hope you will regain your health and again come [in] and work out some truths for the people as this land of U.S. needs such minds to guide U.S. hoping for your good health I am yours Sincerely (I ask otherwise John M. Burnett.
