'Twas such a night as this: There was not a breath of air to ruffle the smooth surface of the calm Adriatic: naught was heard save the distant tinkling of the solitary sheep bell, & the low barking of the honest watchdog. He stood beside a tomb — a deep sigh rent his bosom & even & anon as he cast his tearful, languid eye to the pale, chaste moon, [low] of heaven, he exclaimed: oh Jack! what a night — for catching coons!
