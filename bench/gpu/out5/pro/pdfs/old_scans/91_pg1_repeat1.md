J. A. Andrews / 62 official mess letter offering aid to C. B.

Col. Alexander of Louisville - formerly Maj. Alexander who kept the Soldiers Home in Washington had among other servants - one "Louisa" Rudd. who had been the property of his wife Sallie Rudd - and one Wesley Williams - who became the husband of said Louisa while in Louisville - and they had four children. two of which Mary & Robert they took with them to Washington and two others Rachael and Louisa were left in Louisville with capt. Rudd. or Mrs. Alexander and now with Mrs. Alexander. When Maj. Alexander went to Louisville in 1862 or 3, Louisa & Wesley Williams declined going with him, and remained in Washington.
