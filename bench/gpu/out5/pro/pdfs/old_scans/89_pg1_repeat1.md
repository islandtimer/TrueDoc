62 Letter & request to send Miss Barton to nurse sick & wounded soldiers in Burnside Div.

Commonwealth of Massachusetts. Executive Department. Boston, March 24th 1862

Miss Clara H. Barton North Oxford, Mass'ts Very to assume Gov. Miss Barton, of my cordial sympathy with yr most worthy sentiments & wishes; & that if I have any power to promote yr design in aid of our soldiers I will freely use it.
