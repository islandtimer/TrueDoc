St Louis Jan 13th 1862

Dr Sir.

A friend informs me that a few weeks since, he addressed a letter to me at Louisville, Ky. supposing me to be there. If received, it has probably been advertised. Please forward it to me here &

oblige very respectfully your ob.t sevt J Holt

Dr J. Speed Postmaster Louisville

Learned yesterday you addressed & forwarded a number of letters to you at St. Louis. Resp'y

William L. Kelly Assist PG Jany 15/62 Louisville KY
