Hon. Theodore Roosevelt, Oyster Bay, N. Y. My Dear Honorable: Please pardon the liberty I take in penning you these few lines. I am also enclosing a copy of U. S. Letters patent, that was issued to me Dec. 30th 1913, which is self explanatory. Now what I want to do is this: As I am a poor man, and am not able to finance this proposition; I would like to get an order from you for 10,000, or more, Badge Buttons. The more the better, and a large order such as you might want would enable me to get my proposition on the market.
