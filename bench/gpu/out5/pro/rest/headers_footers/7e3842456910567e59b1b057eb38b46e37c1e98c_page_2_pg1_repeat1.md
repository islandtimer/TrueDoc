10. Cal Grant and Direct Loan Recipients must be enrolled at least half time (6 units) and must have an active academic program (major) other than undecided. See Admissions and Records if updates are necessary.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION FOR STUDENTS UTILIZING FEDERAL FINANCIAL AID

RCCD is excited to announce a new method for receiving your financial aid funds. Starting in July 2014, Higher One will begin mailing bright lime green envelopes which will include a pre-printed Higher One card with the Master Card logo along with step by step instructions on how to sign up for your new disbursement method. Higher One offers students several options:

1. Higher One Card

2. Direct deposit into your bank account

3. Check

Students will need to use the account number on the Higher One card to make a choice of activating the Higher One card, selecting the direct deposit option or requesting a check. It is also important that your mailing address is current to avoid a delay in receiving your Financial Aid funds.
