The distributed middleware architecture of the application was based on the framework described in [10]. The physics of the scene was simulated using the Bullet Physics Library 2 . Simplified forms of non-convex pieces and holes were designed for the physics simulation in order to facilitate their manipulation, as the Bullet Physics Library does not handle non-convex meshes properly.
