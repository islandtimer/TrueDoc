THE FACULTY OF SOCIAL SCIENCES of the University of the West Indies is pleased to be associated with the Institute of Caribbean Studies at the UWI Mona, in presenting the Conference on Caribbean Culture, in honour of distinguished Caribbean Scholar, Professor Rex Nettleford.

The conference will be opened on Sunday, March 3, 1996 by the Rt. Hon. Michael Manley, P.C., and closes with an address by the honoree on Wednesday, March 6, at 8 pm.
