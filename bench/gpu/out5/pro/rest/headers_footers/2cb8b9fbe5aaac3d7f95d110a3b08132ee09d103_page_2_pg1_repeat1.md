2.2. Preparation of MRF. The preparation of MRF can be realized by existing technology [18, 19]. The detailed steps can be described as follows. Firstly, carrier liquid and surfactant are mixed with certain proportion in a container. The mixture is stirred evenly under water-bathing at 50°C in a blender for 2 hours. Then, soft magnetic particles are poured slowly into the container while the blender is running. The particles are evenly distributed in the compound liquid after this step. Secondly, the mixture is milled in a planetary ball mill for about 10 h. The aim of this step is to ensure that soft

magnetic particles are coated sufficiently by surfactant. The whole process can be shown in Figure 2.
