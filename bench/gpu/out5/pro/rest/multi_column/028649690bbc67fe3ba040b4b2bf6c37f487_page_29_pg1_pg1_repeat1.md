DEFINITIONS

Categories of Need

<table><tr><td>Emergency</td><td>Persons needing services immediately.</td></tr><tr><td>Critical</td><td>Persons needing services within one year.</td></tr><tr><td>Planning For</td><td>Persons with needs more than one year away.</td></tr></table>

Categories of Service

<table><tr><td>High Intensity</td><td>Persons receiving residential services, possibly in addition to vocational/day services, ancillary/support services, and case management</td></tr><tr><td>Medium Intensity</td><td>Persons receiving vocational/day services, possibly in addition to support/ancillary services and case management</td></tr><tr><td>Low Intensity</td><td>Persons receiving support or ancillary services (therapies, F.S.S., transportation), in addition to case management services</td></tr></table>

Types of Service

<table><tr><td>Day Services</td><td>Non-residential services to people with mental retardation including adult developmental training, vocational rehabilitation, and community employment</td></tr><tr><td>Residential Services (24 hour staff)</td><td>Services necessary to maintain a person in their home or in some other residential setting by providing staffing support 24 hours a day.</td></tr></table>

<table><tr><td>Residential Services (Not including 24 hour staff)</td><td>Services to people who require some degree of staffing support to maintain them in their home, but do not require staffing on a 24 hour/day continuous basis.</td></tr></table>

<table><tr><td>Fiscal Year</td><td>The 12 month period from July 1 to June 30 on which Commonwealth budgets are based.</td></tr></table>

<table><tr><td>Infrastructure</td><td>The basic foundation that must be in place for an organization to function properly (administrative, information systems, staffing).</td></tr></table>

<table><tr><td>PUNS</td><td>Prioritization of Urgency of Need for Services for Persons with Mental Retardation—Data collection instrument developed by Temple University to assess the need of individuals waiting for services.</td></tr></table>
