To summarize, the conclusion of current research is showing that consuming 3mg/kg caffeine, 1.5 g L-carnitine and their combination has no meaningful effect on performance. More researches are necessary in order to investigate the acute effects of different amount of caffeine and L-carnitine on 400-m freestyle performance time and the blood lactate level.
