$$\begin{array}{ccc} \mathbf{B} & \longrightarrow & \prod_p \widehat{\mathbf{B}} \\ \downarrow & & \downarrow \\ \mathbf{B}_{\mathbf{Q}} & \longrightarrow & \left(\prod_p \widehat{\mathbf{B}}\right)_{\mathbf{Q}}. \end{array}$$

As the functor $\mathrm{Ell}^{\mathrm{or}}(-) \simeq: \mathrm{CAlg} \rightarrow \mathcal{S}$ is corepresented by $\mathrm{TMF}$ when restricted to complex periodic $\mathbf{E}_{\infty}$ -rings by Th.5.4, we have equivalences of spaces

$$\begin{aligned} \mathrm{Ell}^{\mathrm{or}}(\mathbf{B}) \simeq \mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{CAlg}}(\mathrm{TMF}, \mathbf{B}) &\simeq \mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{CAlg}}(\mathrm{TMF}, \mathbf{B}_{\mathbf{Q}}) \times_{\mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{CAlg}}(\mathrm{TMF}, (\prod_p \widehat{\mathbf{B}})_{\mathbf{Q}})} \mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{CAlg}}(\mathrm{TMF}, \prod_p \widehat{\mathbf{B}}) \\ &\simeq \mathrm{Ell}^{\mathrm{or}}(\mathbf{B}_{\mathbf{Q}}) \times_{\mathrm{Ell}^{\mathrm{or}}((\prod_p \widehat{\mathbf{B}})_{\mathbf{Q}}) \simeq} \mathrm{Ell}^{\mathrm{or}}(\prod_p \widehat{\mathbf{B}}) \simeq. \end{aligned}$$

We already have the desired oriented elliptic curves $\widehat{T}$ over each $\widehat{\mathbf{B}} and T_{\mathbf{Q}}$ over $\mathbf{B}_{\mathbf{Q}}$ , so to obtain $T$ over $\mathbf{B}$ , it suffices to produce an isomorphism between oriented elliptic curves over $(\prod_p \widehat{\mathbf{B}})_{\mathbf{Q}}$ . To do this, consider the equivalences of spaces

$$\begin{aligned} \mathrm{Ell}^{\mathrm{or}}\left(\left(\prod_p \widehat{\mathbf{B}}\right)_{\mathbf{Q}}\right) &\simeq \mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{CAlg}}\left(\mathrm{TMF}, \left(\prod_p \widehat{\mathbf{B}}\right)_{\mathbf{Q}}\right) \simeq \mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{CAlg}_{\mathbf{Q}}}\left(\mathrm{TMF}_{\mathbf{Q}}, \left(\prod_p \widehat{\mathbf{B}}\right)_{\mathbf{Q}}\right) \\ &\subseteq \mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{CAlg}_{\mathbf{Q}}}\left(\mathrm{tmf}_{\mathbf{Q}}, \left(\prod_p \widehat{\mathbf{B}}\right)_{\mathbf{Q}}\right) \simeq \Omega^{\infty+8}\left(\left(\prod_p \widehat{\mathbf{B}}\right)_{\mathbf{Q}}\right) \times \Omega^{\infty+12}\left(\left(\prod_p \widehat{\mathbf{B}}\right)_{\mathbf{Q}}\right), \end{aligned} \tag{5.5}$$

where we use that $\mathrm{tmf}_{\mathbf{Q}}$ is the free $\mathbf{E}_{\infty}$ - $\mathbf{Q}$ -algebra on two generators $c_4 and c_6$ in degrees 8 and 12, respectively, that $\mathrm{TMF}_{\mathbf{Q}} \simeq \mathrm{tmf}_{\mathbf{Q}}[\Delta^{-1}]$ for $\Delta = (c_4^3 - c_6^2)/1728$ , and that the containment above is a monomorphism onto those path components where the image of $\Delta$ is invertible. Both the base-change of $\widehat{T} and T_{\mathbf{Q}}$ to $(\prod_p \widehat{\mathbf{B}})_{\mathbf{Q}}$ are chosen such that the associated map from $\mathrm{tmf}_{\mathbf{Q}}$ sends $c_4 and c_6$ to their $q$ -expansion maps. From the equivalences above, this shows that these oriented elliptic curves are equivalent over $(\prod_p \widehat{\mathbf{B}})_{\mathbf{Q}}$ . The fact that $(\prod_p \widehat{\mathbf{B}})_{\mathbf{Q}}$ has homotopy groups concentrated in even degrees also shows that the choice of equivalence is unique up to homotopy; even up to 3-homotopy. This yields the oriented elliptic curve $T$ over $\mathbf{B}$ .

To compute the formal group associated with $T$ , notice that $\mathbf{B}$ is an $\mathbf{E}_{\infty}$ -KU-algebra, as it then follows that the formal group associated with $T$ is $\widehat{\mathbf{G}}_m$ . Indeed, by [Lur18b, Pr.4.3.23], an orientation on $T$ is an equivalence between its associated formal group and the Quillen formal group over $\mathbf{B}$ . As the Quillen formal group of a complex-periodic $\mathbf{E}_{\infty}$ -ring satisfies base-change, we obtain the desired result of the Quillen formal group $\widehat{\mathbf{G}}_m$ over KU.
