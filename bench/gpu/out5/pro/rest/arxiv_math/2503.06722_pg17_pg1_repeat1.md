5. THE INJECTIVE NERVE OF CATEGORIES AND THE REGULAR MAGNITUDE-PATH SPECTRAL SEQUENCE

The goal of this section is construct the regular magnitude-path spectral sequence, in analogy with the magnitude-path spectral sequence developed in [Asa23]. To do so, it shall be more convenient to use the categorical language, and to generalize the framework of eulerian magnitude homology to quivers. In the first subsection we introduce the tool of injective nerves of categories, and then we shall apply it to the reachable categories, getting the regular magnitude-path spectral sequence. As an application of the construction, we shall provide some computations of regular path homology of regularly diagonal graphs.

5.1. The injective nerve. Let $\mathbf{2}$ denote the category consisting of the objects $E and V$ , with two non-identity morphisms $s, t: E \rightarrow V$ called the source and the target . We shall also represent, and identify, a quiver $Q$ by the corresponding directed graph with set of vertices $V$ , and set of edges $E$ . Let $\mathbf{Fin}$ be the full subcategory of $\mathbf{Set}$ of finite sets. By a (finite) quiver we shall mean a functor $Q: \mathbf{2} \rightarrow \mathbf{Fin}$ . Morphisms of quivers are natural transformations of functors. The category $\mathbf{Quiver}$ of finite quivers and morphisms of quivers is the functor category $\mathbf{Fun}(\mathbf{2}, \mathbf{Fin})$ .
