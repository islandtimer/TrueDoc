We are going to prove that a measure with the maximal entropy cannot be carried by a subset of $S_\beta \setminus P_\beta^{\mathbb{Z}}$ .

We define on the tower $(\Omega, T)$ of $P_\beta$ the map $f$ by:

$$f((x_n)_{n \in \mathbb{Z}}, i) = (y_n)_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \quad (70)$$

where $x_k \in \mathfrak{C}_\beta , y_i \in \{0, 1, \dots, d_1\} , y_0$ denotes the $i$ -th letter of $x_0$ .

Remark 6 It is easy to check that $f \circ T = \sigma \circ f$ (see [14]) and $f$ is one to one. Let $(z_n)_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \in P_\beta^{\mathbb{Z}} , (x_n)_{n \in \mathbb{Z}}$ such that $x_n \in P_\beta$ and

$$\cdots x_{-m} \cdots x_{-1} x_0 x_1 x_2 \cdots x_m \cdots = \cdots z_{-n} \cdots z_{-1} z_0 \bullet z_1 \cdots z_n \cdots$$

where $z_0$ is the $i$ -th letter of $x_0$ . The inverse map $f^{-1}$ of $f$ from $P_\beta^{\mathbb{Z}}$ to $\Omega$ is given by

$$f^{-1}((z_n)_{n \in \mathbb{Z}}) = ((x_n)_{n \in \mathbb{Z}}, i). \quad (72)$$

The map $f$ is one to one and $f \circ T = \sigma \circ f$ . Each $\sigma$ -invariant measure $\pi$ on $P_\beta^{\mathbb{Z}}$ generates a measure $\pi \circ f$ on $\Omega$ with the same entropy. Thus the uniqueness of the measure of the entropy $\log |\beta|$ on $\Omega$ implies that there is a unique measure with the same entropy on $P_\beta^{\mathbb{Z}}$ .

4.2.1 Hybrid admissible sequences

Definition 11 In what follows, an admissible sequence $(x_i)_{i \in \mathbb{Z}}$ shall be said hybrid if there is an integer $k \in \mathbb{Z}$ such that one of the following conditions hold:

(a) $(x_i)_{i \geq k} \in \mathfrak{C}_\beta^{\mathbb{N}} and (x_i)_{i < k} \notin \mathfrak{C}_\beta^{\mathbb{N}}$ ; (b) $(x_i)_{i \geq k} \notin \mathfrak{C}_\beta^{\mathbb{N}} and (x_i)_{i < k} \in \mathfrak{C}_\beta^{\mathbb{N}}$ ; (c) $(x_i)_{i \geq k} \in \Delta_{j, \beta}^{\mathbb{N}} and (x_i)_{i < k} \notin \Delta_{j, \beta}^{\mathbb{N}}$ ; (d) $(x_i)_{i \geq k} \notin \Delta_{j, \beta}^{\mathbb{N}} and (x_i)_{i < k} \in \Delta_{j, \beta}^{\mathbb{N}}$ .
