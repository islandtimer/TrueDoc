Proposition 2.2. Let $V \neq \{0\}$ be an ordered normed space whose cone has an interior point $v$ . Then $v$ is an order unit and the order unit norm $\|\cdot\|_v$ is weaker than the original norm $\|\cdot\|$ on $V$ . Moreover, every positive functional on $V$ is continuous with respect to both norms.

Note that the two norms in Proposition 2.2 need not be equivalent, see, e.g., [6, Example 1.5.12]. For a detailed discussion in ordered Banach spaces, see [4, Proposition 2.11].

For an ordered vector space $(V, V_+)$ , a set $S'$ of positive linear functionals from $V$ to $\mathbb{R}$ is said to determine positivity if, for every $v \in V$ , one has $v \geq 0$ if $\varphi(v) \geq 0$ for all $\varphi \in S'$ . Note that, if $V$ is an order unit space and $\Sigma \subseteq V'_+$ is given as in (2.1), then $\text{ext } \Sigma$ determines positivity.

We will make use of the following two simple propositions.

Proposition 2.3. Let $X$ be an ordered normed vector space and let $S' \subseteq X'_+$ . The following are equivalent:

(i) The set $S'$ determines positivity, i.e., for every $x \in X$ , the inequality $\langle x', x \rangle \geq 0$ for all $x' \in S'$ implies that $x \geq 0$ . (ii) The positive-linear hull of $S'$ is weak-* dense in the dual wedge $X'_+$ .

Proof. “(i) $\Rightarrow$ (ii)” We show the contraposition. Let $C' \subseteq X'_+$ denote the weak-* closure of the positive-linear hull of $S'$ and assume that there exists a point $x'_0 \in X'_+$ that is not in $C'$ . By the Hahn–Banach separation theorem in the locally convex space $X'$ with the weak-* topology, there exists a weak-* continuous functional $x'' : X' \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ and a number $\gamma \in \mathbb{R}$ such that $\langle x'', x' \rangle \geq \gamma > \langle x'', x'_0 \rangle$ for all $x' \in C'$ . Due to the weak-* continuity of $x''$ , there exists an element $x \in X$ such that $x''$ is the point evaluation at $x$ , see [13, § IV.1.2 on pp. 124–125]. It follows that $\langle x, x' \rangle \geq \gamma > \langle x, x'_0 \rangle$ for all $x' \in C'$ . Since $0 \in C'$ , we obtain $\gamma \leq 0$ . Therefore, $x$ is not in $X_+$ . As $C'$ is a wedge, for every $x' \in C'$ we have $\langle x, x' \rangle \geq 0$ , hence $C'$ does not determine positivity.

“(ii) $\Rightarrow$ (i)” If $x \in X$ satisfies $\langle x', x \rangle \geq 0$ for all $x' \in S'$ , then it follows from (ii) that the same inequality holds for all $x' \in X'_+$ . As $X_+$ is closed, this implies that $x \in X_+$ . $\square$

The following is a primal version of Proposition 2.3. As its proof is similar, we omit it.

Proposition 2.4. Let $X$ be an ordered normed vector space and let $S \subseteq X_+$ . The following are equivalent:

(i) The set $S$ determines positivity of functionals in the sense that, for each $x' \in X'$ , the inequality $\langle x', x \rangle \geq 0$ for all $x \in S$ implies that $x' \geq 0$ . (ii) The positive-linear hull of $S$ is weakly dense (equivalently, norm dense) in $X_+$ .

If the cone $V_+$ in an ordered vector space $X$ has a base $D$ , then $x \in D$ is extremal if and only if $x$ is an extreme point of $D$ , see [6, Lemma 1.5.19]. The following result by Krein and Milman is given, e.g., in [13, §II.10.5 on p. 68].

Proposition 2.5. Let $V$ be a locally convex Hausdorff space. Let $\Sigma \subseteq V$ be compact and convex and let $S' \subseteq \Sigma$ . If $\Sigma = \overline{\text{conv}(S')}$ then $\text{ext } \Sigma \subseteq \overline{S'}$ .
