Appendix A. Proof of Theorem 3.9.

In this section, we prove Theorem 3.9.

Define $S$ as

$$(A.1) \quad S := \left\{ \{b_i\}_{i=1}^\infty \mid 0 \leq b_i \leq a_i, \sum_{i=1}^\infty b_i = 1 \right\},$$

where $\{a_i\}_{i=1}^\infty$ is a sequence defined in Assumption 3.6. Then, the following lemma holds.

LEMMA A.1. $S$ is a compact subset of $\ell^1(\mathbb{N})$ .

Proof. Let $\{\beta^{(n)}\}_{n=1}^\infty$ be a sequence in $S$ , where $\beta^{(n)} := \{b_i^{(n)}\}_{i=1}^\infty \in S$ . Since $0 \leq b_1^{(n)} \leq a_1$ holds for any $n \in \mathbb{N}$ , the sequence $\{b_1^{(n)}\}_{n=1}^\infty$ has a convergent subsequence $\{b_1^{(1n)}\}_{n=1}^\infty$ . Similarly, since $0 \leq b_2^{(1n)} \leq a_2 , \{b_2^{(1n)}\}_{n=1}^\infty$ also has a convergent subsequence $\{b_2^{(2n)}\}_{n=1}^\infty$ . Repeating this procedure for each coordinate and applying the diagonal argument, we obtain a subsequence $\{\beta^{(n_n)}\}_{n=1}^\infty$ . For all $i \in \mathbb{N}$ , the sequence $\{b_i^{(n_n)}\}_{n=1}^\infty$ converges, and $0 \leq b_i^{(n_n)} \leq a_i$ holds. Define $c_i := \lim_{n \rightarrow \infty} b_i^{(n_n)}$ . Since $0 \leq b_i^{(n_n)} \leq a_i$ , it follows that $|b_i^{(n_n)} - c_i| \leq a_i$ . Moreover, since $\sum_{i=1}^\infty a_i < \infty$ , we can apply Lebesgue convergence theorem. This yields $\lim_{n \rightarrow \infty} \sum_{i=1}^\infty |b_i^{(n_n)} - c_i| = 0$ , which implies that the subsequence $\{\beta^{(n_n)}\}_{n=1}^\infty$ converges to $c$ with respect to $\ell^1$ norm. Furthermore, it is straightforward to verify that $\{c_i\}_{i=1}^\infty \in S$ . Thus, $\{\beta^{(n)}\}_{n=1}^\infty$ has a subsequence that converges in $S$ . Therefore, $S$ is compact. $\square$
