If the sharp Schrödinger admissible condition $\frac{1}{p} = \frac{d}{2}(\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{q})$ holds, then (9) is equivalent to

$$(10) \quad \frac{1}{\beta} \geq \frac{4}{p} - \sigma.$$

$$(11) \quad \frac{1}{\beta} \geq \frac{d}{d-1} \frac{4}{p} - \sigma.$$
