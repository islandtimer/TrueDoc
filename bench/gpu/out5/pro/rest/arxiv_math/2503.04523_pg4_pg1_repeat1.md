2. Geometry of Tucker tensor varieties. In this section, we introduce the preliminaries of variational analysis for low-rank matrices. Then, we describe the notation in tensor operations and Tucker tensor varieties.

Given a nonempty subset $C$ of $\mathbb{R}^{n_1 \times n_2 \times \dots \times n_d}$ , the (Bouligand) tangent cone of $C$ at $\mathcal{X} \in C$ is defined by $T_{\mathcal{X}}C := \{\mathcal{V} \in \mathbb{R}^{n_1 \times n_2 \times \dots \times n_d} : \exists t^{(i)} \rightarrow 0, \mathcal{X}^{(i)} \rightarrow \mathcal{X}$ in $C$ , s. t. $(\mathcal{X}^{(i)} - \mathcal{X})/t^{(i)} \rightarrow \mathcal{V}\}$ . The set $N_{\mathcal{X}}C = (T_{\mathcal{X}}C)^\circ := \{\mathcal{W} \in \mathbb{R}^{n_1 \times n_2 \times \dots \times n_d} : \langle \mathcal{W}, \mathcal{V} \rangle \leq 0 \text{ for all } \mathcal{V} \in T_{\mathcal{X}}C\}$ is called the normal cone of $C$ at $\mathcal{X}$ . Note that if $C$ is a manifold, the tangent cone $T_{\mathcal{X}}C$ (normal cone $N_{\mathcal{X}}C$ ) is a linear space and referred to as the tangent space (normal space). Given a continuously differentiable function $f$ on $C$ , a point $x \in C$ is stationary of $f$ if $\|P_{T_x C}(-\nabla f(x))\|_F = 0$ , or $-\nabla f(x) \in N_x C$ equivalently.
