so that, by the assumption in (5.26b),

$$\limsup_n \left| \int_{t_0}^{t_n} u'_n(t) dt \right|^2 \leq \limsup_n \Gamma(\theta - 1) t_0^{1-\theta} E^\theta(u_n) = 0 ,$$

which contradicts (A.10) and concludes the assertion. $\square$

APPENDIX B. MEASURE-PRESERVING DIFFEOMORPHISMS

Let $(M, g)$ be a smooth connected, orientable Riemannian manifold with Riemannian volume measure $\text{vol}_g$ . We collect here some auxiliary results about the group $\text{Diff}_0^+(g)$ of all compactly non-identical, orientation-preserving, $\text{vol}_g$ -preserving diffeomorphisms.

Firstly, let us recall that $\text{Diff}_0^+(g)$ is the (infinite-dimensional) Lie group corresponding to the Lie algebra of $\text{div}_g$ -free vector fields on $M$ with the Lie derivative as its Lie bracket. Let us further recall some virtually well-known results about the natural action of $\text{Diff}_0^+(g)$ on $M$ .

The following may be easily inferred from the arguments in [13, §3].

Lemma B.1 (Extension lemma). Let $(M, g)$ be in addition open or boundaryless, and $K \subset M$ be any contractible compact subset. Then, every smooth vector field on $K$ has a compactly supported, $\text{div}_g$ -free extension to the whole of $M$ .

As an immediate consequence, we see that the Lie algebra of $\text{div}_g$ -free vector fields is infinite-dimensional, thus so is $\text{Diff}_0^+(g)$ .

Proposition B.2 (Transitivity, [13, Thm. A, §3, p. 98]). $\text{Diff}_0^+(g)$ acts $k$ -transitively on $M$ for every $k \in \mathbb{N}_1$ . In particular, it acts transitively on $M$ .

B.1. Actions on measures. In the following, let $B_1 \subset \mathbb{R}^d$ be the open unit ball equipped with the standard Euclidean metric $g_e$ .

Lemma B.3. Let $\nu$ be any finite measure on $B_1$ invariant for the $(\mathcal{C}_\#)$ -action of $\text{Diff}_0^+(g_e)$ . Then $\nu \propto \mathcal{L}^d|_{B_1}$ . In particular, $\nu$ has no singular continuous part.

Proof. Let $Q_1, Q_2 \subset B_1$ be arbitrary closed cubes with equal volume, define $K$ as the closed convex hull of $Q_1 \cup Q_2$ , and let $w$ be the vector field on $K$ defining the translation of $Q_1$ to $Q_2$ . By Lemma B.1 applied to $K and w$ , there exists a compactly supported $\text{div}_{g_e}$ -free vector field $w'$ on $M$ extending $w$ on $K$ . Then, the flow of $w'$ at time 1 is a compactly non-identical orientation-preserving, $\mathcal{L}^d$ -preserving diffeomorphism on $M$ mapping $Q_1$ to $Q_2$ . In other words, $\text{Diff}_0^+(g_e)$ acts transitively on all closed cubes in $B_1$ , hence, by continuity, on the family $\mathcal{C}$ of all semi-closed cubes $Q$ of the form $Q := x + q[0, 1)^d$ with $x \in \mathbb{R}^d , q \in \mathbb{Q}^+$ and such that $\overline{Q} \subset B_1$ .

Since $\nu$ is invariant, by a standard decomposition argument, for every rational $q \in \mathbb{Q}^+$ and every $Q \in \mathcal{C}$ with $qQ \in \mathcal{C}$ we have $\nu(qQ) = q^d \nu Q$ . Now, let $Q_0 \in \mathcal{C}$ be fixed, and set $c := \nu Q_0 / \mathcal{L}^d Q_0$ . For every $Q_1 \in \mathcal{C}$ there exists $q \in \mathbb{Q}^+ and x \in \mathbb{R}^d$ so that $Q_1 = x + qQ_0$ . For such $q$ , by invariance of $\nu$ and of $\mathcal{L}^d$ (also under rescaling),

$$\nu Q_1 = \nu(qQ_0) = q^d \nu Q_0 = q^d c \mathcal{L}^d Q_0 = c \mathcal{L}^d(qQ_0) = c \mathcal{L}^d Q_1 .$$

Since $\mathcal{C}$ is a $\pi$ -system generating the Borel $\sigma$ -algebra of $B_1$ , it follows by a standard monotone class argument that $\nu = c \mathcal{L}^d$ as Borel measures on $B_1$ , which concludes the proof. $\square$
