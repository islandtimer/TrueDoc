(i) $\vec{t} \in R_{int}(\vec{r}; \vec{k}')$ : Note that we have either $v_p(\Theta_0) \notin \{0, v_p(x_0)\}$ or $v_p(\Theta_0) \notin \{-1, -1 - v_p(x_1)\}$ , in this case. So, $\overline{\rho}^{ss}|_{I_{\mathbf{Q}_{p^2}}} \cong \omega_2^{p+1} \oplus \omega_2^{p+1}$ ,
