Corollary 11.7. Suppose that $f' = \sum_{\tau} p_{\tau,!}(f^{\tau}) and \tilde{f}$ is a nice transfer. Then we have the identity

$$\frac{1}{2^{1-|S|}L(0, \eta)} \sum_{\tau \in \mathcal{V}_n(E/F)} \sum_{\Pi} J_{\Pi}^{\tau, \tau_0}(f^{\tau}) = \sum_{\pi} I_{\pi}(\tilde{f}),$$

where the left-hand sum ranges over irreducible cuspidal representations $\Pi$ of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_E)$ with central character $\omega'$ and the right-hand sum ranges over irreducible cuspidal automorphic representations of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_F)$ with central character $\omega$ or $\omega\eta$ .

To obtain the necessary refined comparison, we make another use of [Ram18, Theorem A]. We introduce the following terminology to simplify a few statements.

Definition 11.8. Fix a character $\omega$ of $Z(\mathbb{A}_F)$ and set $\omega' = \omega \circ \mathrm{Nm}_{E/F}$ . Fix a split place $v_1$ and a supercuspidal representation $\pi_{v_1}$ of $\mathrm{GL}_n(F_{v_1})$ . We say that the functions

$$\tilde{f} \in C_c^{\infty}(\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_F) \times \mathbb{A}_{F,n}) \text{ and } \{f^{\tau}\}_{\tau \in \mathcal{V}_n(E/F)} \subset C_c^{\infty}(\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_E))$$

are efficient transfers for $(\omega, \pi_{v_1})$ if they give a $\omega$ -nice transfer such that $f_{v_1} \in C_c^{\infty}(\mathrm{GL}_n(F_{v_1}))_{\Omega}$ where $\Omega$ is the block of $\pi_{v_1}$ ( $f_{v_1}$ is said to be essentially a matrix coefficient of $\pi_{v_1}$ in this case) and that $f\tau_{n v_1}$ is related to $f_{v_1}$ as in Proposition 9.12; note that this implies that $f_{v_1}^{\tau}$ is essentially a matrix coefficient of the base change $\Pi_{v_1} \simeq \pi_{v_1} \otimes \pi_{v_1}$ of $\pi_{v_1}$ .

Making use of the strong multiplicity one theorem [Ram18, Theorem A], we may separate the terms in the preceding sum. For a cuspidal automorphic representation $\Pi$ of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_E)$ , we denote by $\mathcal{B}(\Pi)$ the (finite) set of cuspidal automorphic representations $\pi$ of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_F)$ such that $\Pi = \mathrm{BC}_E(\pi)$ . We have the following comparison of relative characters.

Proposition 11.9. For almost all split places $v$ , we fix an irreducible unramified representation $\pi_v^0$ . For a fixed split place $v_1$ , we fix a supercuspidal representation $\pi_{v_1}$ of $\mathrm{GL}_n(F_{v_1})$ . Then there exists at most one cuspidal automorphic representation $\Pi$ of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_E)$ such that if $\tilde{f} and \{f^{\tau}\}_{\tau \in \mathcal{V}_n(E/F)}$ are efficient transfers for $(\omega, \pi_{v_1})$ , then

$$\frac{1}{2^{1-|S|}L(0, \eta)} \sum_{\tau \in \mathcal{V}_n(E/F)} J_{\Pi}^{\tau, \tau_0}(f^{\tau}) = \sum_{\pi \in \mathcal{B}(\Pi)} I_{\pi}(\tilde{f}),$$

where the sum on the right runs over all cuspidal automorphic representations $\pi$ of $\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathbb{A}_F)$ such that

(1) $\pi_v \cong \pi_v^0$ for almost all split $v$ , (2) $\pi_{v_1}$ is our fixed supercuspidal representation,

and where $\Pi = \mathrm{BC}_E(\pi)$ is the base change of any $\pi$ appearing in the sum.

Remark 11.10. Note that the comparison above is vacuous unless $\pi = \bigotimes_v \pi_v$ is $\mathrm{GL}_n$ -elliptic at some split place $v_2$ (this is built into the requirement that $\tilde{f} and \{f^{\tau}\}_{\tau \in \mathcal{V}_n(E/F)}$ are efficient transfers). By Lemma 11.16, it suffices for $\pi_{v_2}$ to be supercuspidal.
