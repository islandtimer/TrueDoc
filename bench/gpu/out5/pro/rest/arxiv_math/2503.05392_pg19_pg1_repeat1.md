3.1.2. Results for the $L_\psi$ general affine surface areas. In the case of the $L_\psi$ general affine surface areas, we deduce the following results. Their proofs follow in completely analogous fashion to those for the $L_\phi$ general surface areas in the results above, and hence are omitted.

Theorem 3.6. Let $\psi \in \text{Conv}(0, \infty) and a, b \geq 0$ , with $a, b$ not both zero. Suppose that the function $G(t) = \psi(t^{n+1})$ is convex for $t \in (0, \infty)$ . Let $K_1, K_2 \in \mathcal{K}_o^n$ be such that $K_1|e_n^\perp = K_2|e_n^\perp$ , and assume that $K_1, K_2$ have positive Gaussian curvature almost everywhere. Then

$$\text{as}_\psi(a \diamond_{e_n} K_1 \oplus_{e_n} b \diamond_{e_n} K_2) \leq \frac{a}{a+b} \cdot \text{as}_\psi((a+b) \diamond_{e_n} K_1) + \frac{b}{a+b} \cdot \text{as}_\psi((a+b) \diamond_{e_n} K_2).$$

In the special case $a + b = 1$ , we obtain the following

Corollary 3.3. Let $\psi \in \text{Conv}(0, \infty) and \lambda \in [0, 1]$ be given. Suppose that the function $G(t) = \psi(t^{n+1})$ is convex for $t \in (0, \infty)$ . Let $K_1, K_2 \in \mathcal{K}_o^n$ be such that $K_1|e_n^\perp = K_2|e_n^\perp$ , and assume that $K_1, K_2$ have positive Gaussian curvature almost everywhere. Then we have

$$\text{as}_\psi(\lambda \diamond_{e_n} K_1 \oplus_{e_n} (1-\lambda) \diamond_{e_n} K_2) \leq \lambda \text{as}_\psi(K_1) + (1-\lambda) \text{as}_\psi(K_2).$$

Remark 3.4. Let $K \in \mathcal{K}_o^n and \psi \in \text{Conv}(0, \infty)$ , and suppose that the function $G(t) = \phi(t^{n+1})$ is convex for $t \in (0, \infty)$ . Ye [49, Theorem 3.10] proved that if $K$ has positive Gaussian curvature almost everywhere, then

$$\text{as}_\psi(K) \geq \text{as}_\psi(S_{e_n}(K)).$$

Again recalling that $\frac{1}{2} \diamond_{e_n} K \oplus_{e_n} \frac{1}{2} \diamond_{e_n} R_{e_n}(K_1) = S_{e_n}(K)$ , we see that Corollary 3.3 extends [49, Theorem 3.10] from the case of the Steiner symmetral of a single convex body to the graph sum of two convex bodies.

Corollary 3.4. Let $\psi \in \text{Conv}(0, \infty)$ be given. Let $K_1, K_2 \in \mathcal{K}_o^n$ be convex bodies with curvature functions and which satisfy $K_1|e_n^\perp = K_2|e_n^\perp$ . If the function $G(t) = \psi(t^{n+1})$ is convex for $t \in (0, \infty)$ , then

$$\lambda \text{as}_\psi^*(K_1) + (1-\lambda) \text{as}_\psi^*(K_2) \geq \text{as}_\psi^*([\lambda \diamond_{e_n} K_1^\circ \oplus_{e_n} (1-\lambda) \diamond_{e_n} K_2^\circ]^\circ).$$

Remark 3.5. Let $\psi \in \text{Conv}(0, \infty)$ and suppose that $G(t) = \psi(t^{n+1})$ is convex for $t \in (0, \infty)$ . Ye [49, Corollary 3.11] proved that for every $K \in \mathcal{K}_o^n$ with a curvature function and for which $[S_{e_n}(K^\circ)]^\circ$ has a curvature function, we have

$$\text{as}_\psi^*(K) \geq \text{as}_\psi^*([S_{e_n}(K^\circ)]^\circ).$$

Thus, in the special case $K_2 = R_{e_n}(K_1)$ , Corollary 3.4 reduces to [49, Corollary 3.11].
