A subrepresentation of a representation $V$ of the magnetic point group $(G, \phi)$ is a subspace $W \subset V$ such that the restriction of the $G$ -action to $W$ is also a representation of $(G, \phi)$ . A representation $V$ of $(G, \phi)$ is irreducible if its only representations are $V$ and $0$ .

Now, the category of representations of finite magnetic groups is semisimple.

Proposition 1.9. Every representation of a finite magnetic group is a sum of irreducible representations.

Which follows from Maschke's lemma applied to representations of magnetic groups.

Lemma 1.10 (Maschke). If $W$ is a subrepresentation of $V$ of a finite magnetic group, then there exists a subrepresentation $W'$ of $V$ such that $V = W \oplus W'$ .

Proof. Write $V$ as a direct sum $V = W \oplus U$ (with $U$ not necessary a subrepresentation) and consider the projection $\pi_1 : V \rightarrow W$ given by $\pi_1(w + u) = w$ . Define

$$(1.10) \quad \begin{aligned} \pi : V &\longrightarrow W \\ v &\longmapsto \sum_{g \in G} g \cdot \pi_1(g^{-1} \cdot v). \end{aligned}$$

The morphism $\pi$ is in $\text{Hom}_{\mathbf{Rep}(G, \phi)}(V, W)$ because if $h \in G and v \in V$ , then

$$\begin{aligned} (1.11) \quad \pi(h \cdot v) &= \sum_{g \in G} g \cdot \pi_1(g^{-1} \cdot (h \cdot v)) \\ (1.12) \quad &= \sum_{g \in G} g \cdot \pi_1((g^{-1}h) \cdot v) \\ (1.13) \quad &= \sum_{g \in G} (hh^{-1}g) \cdot \pi_1((h^{-1}g)^{-1} \cdot v) \\ (1.14) \quad &= h \cdot \sum_{g \in G} (h^{-1}g) \cdot \pi_1((h^{-1}g)^{-1} \cdot v) \\ (1.15) \quad &= h \cdot \sum_{l \in G} l \cdot \pi_1(l^{-1} \cdot v) \\ (1.16) \quad &= h \cdot \pi(v) \end{aligned}$$

The morphism $\pi$ is surjective, this follows since $\pi_1(w) = w$ for $w \in W$ , so $\pi(w) = |G|w$ . If we denote by $W' = \ker \pi$ , then we have the decomposition as a direct sum of representations of $(G, \phi)$

$$(1.17) \quad V \cong \text{Im } \pi \oplus \ker \pi = W \oplus W'.$$

□

One key step required to classify the irreducible representations of magnetic groups $(G, \phi)$ is to construct the induced representations of the core $G_0 \leq G$ .

Definition 1.11. Let $(G, \phi)$ be a finite magnetic point group and $V$ a representation of the core group $G_0$ . The induced representation of $V$ is the complex vector space

$$(1.18) \quad \text{Ind}_{G_0}^{(G, \phi)} V := \mathbb{C}[G, \phi] \otimes_{\mathbb{C}[G_0]} V$$
