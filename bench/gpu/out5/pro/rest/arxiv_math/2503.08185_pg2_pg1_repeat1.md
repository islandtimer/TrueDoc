where $\text{Ent}_\pi(f^2) = \mathbb{E}_\pi[f^2 \log f^2] - \mathbb{E}_\pi[f^2] \log \mathbb{E}_\pi[f^2]$ is the entropy of $f^2$ , and where $\mathcal{E}_P(f, f)$ is the Dirichlet form defined by

$$\mathcal{E}_P(f, f) = \sum_{x, y \in \Omega} \pi(x) P(x, y) (f(x) - f(y)) f(x).$$

A classical result is that

$$t_{\text{mix}}(P, \varepsilon) \leq \left\lceil C_{\text{LS}}(P) \left( \log \log \left( \frac{1}{\pi_*} \right) + 2 \log \left( \frac{1}{\varepsilon} \right) \right) \right\rceil,$$

where $\pi_* = \min_{x \in \Omega} \pi(x)$ (see for instance [5] and [11]). Since in our case we have $\pi_* \asymp \frac{1}{2^{n^2}}$ , we see that Theorem 1 will follow from the following proposition.

Proposition 2. The logarithmic Sobolev constant of $P$ satisfies

$$C_{\text{LS}}(P) \lesssim n^2.$$

In the above proposition and in the rest of the paper, the notation $u_n \lesssim v_n$ means that there exists an absolute constant $c > 0$ such that for all $n \geq 1$ , we have $u_n \leq cv_n$ .

Interestingly, a crucial ingredient in the proof of Proposition 2 is the bound of $O(n)$ on the Poincaré constant (inverse of the spectral gap), obtained by Kassabov [9].

Remark 1. Proposition 2 even yields an upper bound of $O(n^2 \log n)$ for the $\ell^2$ -mixing time, at least for the continuous-time and lazy versions of the chain.

We also study another chain on $\Omega$ , whose kernel is denoted by $Q$ , and whose dynamics is as follows: if the current state is a matrix $x \in \Omega$ with rows $\ell_1, \dots, \ell_n$ , then an index $i$ between 1 and $n$ is chosen uniformly at random, and row $\ell_i$ is replaced by $\ell_i + \sum_{j \neq i} a_j \ell_j$ , with $(a_j)_{j \neq i}$ independent Bernoulli random variables with parameter $1/2$ . In other words, the chain $Q$ randomizes a uniformly chosen row, conditionally on the event that the resulting matrix is invertible (it is the Gibbs sampler for the joint distributions of rows). The following proposition shows that the mixing time of $Q$ lies between $n \log n and 4n \log n$ whatever the value of the threshold $\varepsilon$ , establishing a pre-cutoff.

Proposition 3. For all $\varepsilon \in (0, 1)$ , there exists a constant $c_\varepsilon > 0$ such that

$$n \log n - c_\varepsilon n \leq t_{\text{mix}}(Q, \varepsilon) \leq 4n \log n - 2n \log \log n + c_\varepsilon n.$$

In particular, the chain $Q$ has a pre-cutoff.

2. RELATED WORK

To our knowledge, the chain $P$ was first studied by Diaconis and Saloff-Coste [6], who used a comparison with another chain on $\Omega$ studied by Hildebrand [8] to get an upper bound of $O(n^2)$ on the relaxation time (the inverse of the spectral gap), implying an upper bound of $O(n^4)$ on the $\ell^2$ -mixing time. Then the results of Kassabov [9] on the Kazhdan constant yield an upper bound of $O(n^3)$ on the $\ell^2$ -mixing time, which was the best known upper bound for total variation mixing as well.
