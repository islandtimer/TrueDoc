2.2. The compact Kähler case. An important class of compact manifolds are those admitting a Kähler metric. This includes all complex submanifolds of projective space.

In this case, the cohomological story greatly simplifies: Roughly speaking, all cohomologies are determined by Dolbeault cohomology. More precisely:

Proposition 2.3 (The $\partial\bar{\partial}$ -Lemma, [DGMS75]). For any bicomplex $A = (A, \partial, \bar{\partial})$ , the following assertions are equivalent:

(1) For any $a \in A$ such that $\partial a = \bar{\partial} a = 0 and a = db$ for some $b \in A$ , there exists a $c \in A$ such that $a = \partial\bar{\partial}c$ . (2) There is an isomorphism $A \cong A^{sq} \oplus A^{dot}$ , where $A^{sq}$ is a direct sum of squares, i.e. bicomplexes of the form

$$(2.3) \quad \begin{array}{ccc} \mathbb{C} & \longrightarrow & \mathbb{C} \\ \uparrow & & \uparrow \\ \mathbb{C} & \longrightarrow & \mathbb{C}, \end{array}$$

where all arrows are $\pm \text{id}$ and all other maps vanish, and $A^{dot}$ is a direct sum of dots, i.e. one-dimensional bicomplexes with all differentials being zero.

(3) All maps in the diagram

$$(2.4) \quad \begin{array}{ccccc} & & H_{BC}(A) & & \\ & \swarrow & \downarrow & \searrow & \\ H_{\bar{\partial}}(A) & \xRightarrow{\quad} & H_{dR}(A) & \xLeftarrow{\quad} & H_{\partial}(A) \\ & \searrow & \downarrow & \swarrow & \\ & & H_A(A) & & \end{array}$$

are isomorphisms.

(4) The spectral sequences in the previous diagram degenerate, and the filtrations on de Rham cohomology are $n$ -opposed, i.e. $b_k^{p,q} = 0$ unless $k = p + q$ .

Moreover, for a compact Kähler manifold $X$ , the bicomplex $A_X$ satisfies these conditions.

A complex manifold $X$ for which $A_X$ satisfies the above conditions is called a $\partial\bar{\partial}$ -manifold. Compact Kähler manifolds are $\partial\bar{\partial}$ -manifolds, but the converse is not true. A broader class is for example given by those manifolds bimeromorphic to compact Kähler manifolds, i.e. Fujiki's class $\mathcal{C}$ , but also these do not exhaust all $\partial\bar{\partial}$ -manifolds, see e.g. [Fri19], [Li24], [KS23a].
