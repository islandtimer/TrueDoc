4 Proof of Theorem 1.5

The proof proceeds by induction. If $k^{\log_2 3} \leq n \leq 36k^{\log_2 3}$ , then

$$e(G) \leq 3n - 6 \leq 3n - 6 - \frac{n}{36k^{1+\log_2 3}} + \frac{k^3}{4},$$

the result holds. So, we assume that

$$n > 36k^{\log_2 3}. \quad (2)$$

Let $G$ be a $2C_k$ -free plane graph with $e(G) = ex_{\mathcal{P}}(n, 2C_k)$ . It is clear that $G$ is connected; otherwise we can add an edge between two components of $G$ to make it remain $2C_k$ -free, a contradiction. If $G$ is $C_k$ -free, then by Theorem 1.3, $e(G) \leq 3n - 6 - \frac{n}{4k^{\log_2 3}} \leq 3n - 6 - \frac{n}{36k^{1+\log_2 3}} + \frac{k^2}{2}$ , the result holds. So, we assume that $G$ contains a $C_k$ .

Case 1. $G$ is 2-connected.

In that case, the boundary of each face in $G$ is a cycle. Assume that $c$ is the smallest number of vertices that need to be removed from $G$ to make it $C_k$ -free, and let $v_1, v_2, \dots, v_c$ be one such set of vertices. Then $c \leq k$ , since we can remove vertices of a $k$ -cycle to make it $C_k$ -free. Moreover, for any $i, j \in [c]$ , there exists a $k$ -cycle in $G$ such that $v_i$ is contained in the $k$ -cycle, but $v_j$ not.
