Lemma 8.1.6. Suppose $\ell \neq 2 and (\ell, n!) = 1$ if we are in case O1. If $\phi \in \Phi^{\text{ss}}(G^*, \overline{\mathbb{F}}_\ell)$ is a generic semi-simple toral $L$ -parameter, and $\mu \in X_\bullet(G^*)$ is a dominant cocharacter, then $\phi$ is $\mu$ -regular.

Similarly, if $\phi^\# \in \Phi^{\text{ss}}(G^\#, \overline{\mathbb{F}}_\ell)$ is a generic semi-simple toral $L$ -parameter, and $\mu^\# \in X_\bullet(G^\#)$ is a dominant cocharacter such that $(\ell, n!) = 1$ if we are in case O1, then $\phi^\#$ is $\mu^\#$ -regular.

In particular, $\mu^\#$ can be chosen to be not fixed by any non-trivial element of $W_{G^\#}$ .

Proof. For general linear groups (i.e., in case U) this is proved in [HL24, Lemma 4.22], and the argument also works for $G \in \{\text{SO}(d(G)), \text{GSpin}(d(G))\}$ :

The standard representation extends to a standard representation of $\text{GSp}(2n(G^*))$ or $\text{GSO}(2n(G^*))$ , and we write the highest weight of the standard representation of $\text{GSp}(2n(G^*))$ or $\text{GSO}(2n(G^*))$ as $\omega_1^\#$ . Thus, they are also $\mu$ -regular (resp. $\mu^\#$ -regular) by the proof of [Ham24, Theorem 10.10].

For other cocharacters, we first recall that with $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell$ -coefficients, the highest weight tilting module $\mathcal{T}_{\omega_i}$ associated to $\omega_i \in X_\bullet(\text{Sp}(2n(G^*)))$ is realized on the space of harmonic elements in $\wedge^i(\widehat{\text{Std}})$ as defined in [GW09, §5.5.2], and it extends to a standard representation of $\text{GSp}(2n(G^*))$ with highest weight denoted by $\omega_i^\#$ , and the same is true with $\overline{\mathbb{F}}_\ell$ -coefficients, where each highest weight tilting module associated to $\omega_i^\#$ of $\text{GSp}(2n(G^*))$ is a direct sum of $\wedge^i(\widehat{\text{Std}})$ , by our assumption on $\ell$ , cf. [Jan03, Page 286-287] and [Ham24, §10.1, Appendix B.2]. $$G_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_p}^\# \cong \begin{cases} \text{GL}(1)_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_p} \times \prod_{v \in \text{Hom}(K, \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_p)} \text{GSpin}(V^* \otimes_{K,v} \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_p) & \text{in case O} \\ \text{GL}(1)_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_p} \times \prod_{v \in \text{Hom}(K, \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_p)} \text{GU}(V^* \otimes_{K_1,v} \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_p) & \text{in case U} \end{cases},$$
