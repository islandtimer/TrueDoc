depends on the separation $\delta$ . Therefore, if for each $w \in \mathcal{D}_{W,r}$ the marked points of $\Gamma_{\text{out}}^{*,B(w,2r),B(w,r)}$ are $\delta$ -separated, then the conditional probability that the resampling is $\mathfrak{F}_{W,r}$ -successful is positive and uniformly bounded from below.

Lemma 5.4. Fix $W \subseteq D and r > 0$ . For any $M_0 \in \mathbf{N} , \delta > 0$ , there exists $p > 0$ such that the following is true. Suppose that $\Gamma$ is a multichordal $\text{CLE}_\kappa$ in $(D; \underline{x}; \beta) \in \mathfrak{D}_{2N}^{\text{ext}}$ . Apply the resampling procedure described just above to $\Gamma$ . Let $E_\delta$ be the event that for every $w \in \mathcal{D}_{W,r}$ we have that $\Gamma_{\text{out}}^{*,B(w,2r),B(w,r)}$ is $\delta$ -separated. Then for any choice of target pivots $\mathfrak{F}_{W,r} = \{(\ell_i^1, \ell_i^2, y_i)\}_{i=1,\dots,M^*}$ with $M^* \leq M_0$ we have

$$\mathbf{P}[G_{W,r}^{\text{res}} \mid \Gamma] \mathbf{1}_{E_\delta} \geq p \mathbf{1}_{E_\delta}$$

where $G_{W,r}^{\text{res}}$ is the event that the resampling is $\mathfrak{F}_{W,r}$ -successful.

Proof. Suppose we are given any target pivots $\mathfrak{F}_{W,r} = \{(\ell_i^1, \ell_i^2, y_i)\}_{i=1,\dots,M^*}$ with $M^* \leq M_0$ . If we perform the resampling operation, then with uniformly positive probability (depending on $M_0, r$ ) we sample $\widetilde{M} = M^*$ and the points $w_i \in \mathcal{D}_{W,r/2}$ such that $w_i \in B(y_i, r/2)$ for every $i = 1, \dots, M^*$ . In particular, the strands $\ell_i^1, \ell_i^2$ cross the annulus $A(w_i, r, 2r)$ . On the event $E_\delta$ the strands of $\Gamma_{\text{out}}^{*,B(w_i,2r),B(w_i,r)}$ are $\delta$ -separated. Therefore, by Corollary 3.16, the probability that $\ell_i^1, \ell_i^2$ switch their linking pattern after performing the resampling is bounded from below uniformly in $\delta$ . Moreover, by continuity (Theorem 1.7) we see that with positive probability bounded from below the new strands $\ell_i^{\text{res},1}, \ell_i^{\text{res},2}$ intersect again inside $B(w_i, r/2)$ . (Although we have not phrased the topology (1.2) to keep track of the intersection points between the chords, the proof of Theorem 1.7 shows that continuity also holds when we require the intersection sets to converge.) Repeating this argument $M^*$ times for each target pivotal, we conclude. $\square$
