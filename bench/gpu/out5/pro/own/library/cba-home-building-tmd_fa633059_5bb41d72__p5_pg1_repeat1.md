Eligibility criteria for the Product

To purchase this product, customers will need to satisfy certain eligibility criteria, including that customers must:

• be individuals aged 18 years and above (unless by exception), trustees or a company;

• own a residential building in Australia;

• agree to Commonwealth Bank of Australia (CBA) and Hollard exchanging their personal information; and

• meet Hollard’s underwriting guidelines, which outline specific policy acceptance criteria. Some factors which may mean a customer or their home does not meet Hollard’s underwriting guidelines will be reviewed at the time of application or renewal, and may include the customer’s:

○ particular address;

○ requested Building sum insured;

○ criminal history (such as fraud or dishonesty);

☐ claims history (claims made prior to and during the period of insurance); and

○ insurance history (such as previous insurance declined).

If a customer does not satisfy the eligibility criteria then they are outside the target market.

Customers outside of the Target Market

The following types of customers will fall outside the target market for this product. Those who:

• do not own a residential property as an owner occupier;

• are in the course of constructing a residential property;

• require cover for loss or damage caused by building works while undergoing construction or renovations;

• want full terms of cover as per the PDS if the residential property will be unoccupied for 60 consecutive days or more;

• use the property primarily for business, commercial or income-producing activities; or

• require insurance for:

○ a strata, community or company titled property;

○ a rental property;

○ farms, or hobby farms, or like sites, (unless Hollard have stated on the Certificate of Insurance that the property is a hobby farm);

☐ a building that comprises apartments, units or flats;

○ shipping containers, caravans and similar vehicles;

○ a boarding house, Government owned residence, hostel or hotel, mobile or moveable home, a building located in a caravan or holiday park; and

○ a property that has not been well maintained nor watertight, structurally sound or secure.
