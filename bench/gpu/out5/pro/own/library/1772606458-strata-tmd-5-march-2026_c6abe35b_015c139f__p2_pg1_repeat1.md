Likely objectives, financial situation and needs of customers in the target market

Customers in the target market for the Product will likely have a need to manage their financial exposure for loss or damage to their strata building, as well as associated costs and expenses. They are also likely to need cover for their legal liability in relation to the personal injury or property damage of others, for the legal liability of office bearers and for loss or damage to landlord's and common area contents.

Customers in the target market will therefore have the likely objective of transferring a material portion of that financial exposure through insurance protection of the types covered by this Product.

The likely financial situation of customers in the target market will be that they can afford to pay the premiums for the Product, bear the excess/es that apply to a claim and financially contribute to any amounts for loss, damage or liability that are not covered by the Product.
