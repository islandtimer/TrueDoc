Example 2: Total loss, removal of debris, other repair/rebuilding costs, loss of rent

You have insured your property for $350,000. You have insured your contents consisting of carpets, curtains and blinds for $20,000. There is a fire at your property and we determine that your property is a total loss. Your property was rented to a paying tenant at the time of the fire. A property excess of $500 and a contents excess of $500 applies to your policy.
