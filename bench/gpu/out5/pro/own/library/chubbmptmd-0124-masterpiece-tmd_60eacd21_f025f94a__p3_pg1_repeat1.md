2. Product Target Market

What is the Product?

This Product is a residential building and contents insurance policy, for people with assets to insure, offering a premium level of cover for physical loss or damage to their residential building and/or contents and valuables. This Product also provides additional covers for the family such as personal cyber cover and personal legal liability cover.

Key covers/attributes

✓ This Product provides financial protection for physical loss or damage occurring to:

• a residential building;

• personal property items such as jewellery, electronics and furniture;

• worldwide cover for valuable articles such as jewellery, fine art and collectibles;

- business property items up to $50,000 for most professions and up to $75,000 for doctors, lawyers, architects and veterinarians who conduct business from their residential properties.

✓ This Product also offers Family Protection for:

• cyber extortion, financial loss or bullying;

• car-jacking;

• hijacking;

• child abduction;

• aggravated assault; and

• terrorism and active assailants.

✓ Personal Liability Coverage for legal liability anywhere in the world (subject to international sanctions).

Key exclusions

This Product excludes claims arising from (amongst other things):

• flood and action of the sea unless specifically noted in your policy documentation;

• wear and tear;

• loss or damage to contents caused by repairs and renovations;
