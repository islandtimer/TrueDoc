No insurance covers everything so it is important to understand the exclusions of your policy. These exclusions apply to your chosen cover.

What is not covered under your policy?

Exclusions

The following General exclusions apply to all covers under your policy.

You are not covered for any loss or damage caused by or resulting from, or the costs incurred from or of:

bushfire, storm, rainwater or flood which occurs within the first 72 hours of the start of your policy unless you transferred a building and/or contents insurance policy with equivalent cover to us from another insurance company without an interruption in cover. We will not cover any increase in sums insured for these events in the first 72 hours;

your building being unoccupied for longer than 90 consecutive days. This exclusion will not apply to loss or damage caused by lightning or earthquake that occurs during the period of unoccupancy;

■ actions of the sea, including high tides;

gradual deterioration, including wear and tear, rust, corrosion, depreciation, fading or deterioration from exposure to light;
