Update pursuant to ASIC Corporations Instrument 2016/1055

Below are non-materially adverse updates to the AAMI Strata Insurance Product Disclosure Statement prepared on 15 October 2024 ("PDS").

What is the change?

1. On page 78, of the PDS, in the section titled 'Limit' for Office bearer's liability the amount of $1,000 is deleted and replaced with $1,000,000. This means that the most we'll pay under the 'Office bearer's liability' cover in total for all claims in the period of insurance (including legal costs) is $1,000,000.

2. On page 82 of the PDS, the last row in the table 'The benefits for Voluntary workers cover', on the most we will pay for all payments under Voluntary workers cover is deleted and replaced with the following:

‘The most we pay for all payments made under this additional cover is:

• for each incident causing a claim

$208,000

• all incidents causing a claim in any one period of insurance

$1,000,000'
