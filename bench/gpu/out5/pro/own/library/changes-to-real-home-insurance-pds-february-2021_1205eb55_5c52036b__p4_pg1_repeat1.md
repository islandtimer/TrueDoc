Changes to the limits:

The limit for the Optional benefit ‘Rent default and Legal expenses’ for our Landlords insurance product has been reduced. From the effective date, the new level of cover for rent default is the lesser of, up to 6 weeks of rent, or $2,500. Associated legal expenses will now be included in this limit.

Other changes to our wording

We have also made other changes to clarify the policy wording. These changes are not intended to affect the cover provided.
