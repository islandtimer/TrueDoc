Example 3: Partial loss – Loss of rent – tenant default

Your home has a sum insured of $260,000. Your tenant signed a 12 month written rental agreement where the weekly rent payable is $350. Your tenant leaves the home without notice and owes you 12 weeks of rent until the end of the rental agreement. That same tenant also deliberately put a hole in one of the walls of your home. It takes you 10 weeks to secure a suitable replacement tenant. Your home excess is $500.
