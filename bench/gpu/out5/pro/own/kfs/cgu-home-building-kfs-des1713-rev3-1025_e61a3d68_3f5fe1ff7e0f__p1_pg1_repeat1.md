Step 1 Understanding the Facts Sheet

This Key Facts Sheet sets out some of the events covered and not covered by this policy and other information you should consider. This sheet does not provide a complete statement of the cover offered, exclusions, conditions and limits that apply under the policy. You should carefully read the Product Disclosure Statement (PDS) and all relevant policy documentation for more details.

Step 2 Check the maximum level of cover and the events covered

Under this policy: You set the maximum level of cover and your payout is limited to that amount (sum insured). The insurer may provide some cover above this amount.
