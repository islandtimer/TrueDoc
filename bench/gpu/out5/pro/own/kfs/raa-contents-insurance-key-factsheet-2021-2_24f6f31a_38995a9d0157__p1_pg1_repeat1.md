Step 1 Understanding the Facts Sheet

This Key Facts Sheet provides an outline of some of the key facts and information you should take into consideration prior to entering into this policy. This Key Facts Sheet may also be used to compare this policy with other similar policies to ensure your insurance needs are met. This Key Facts Sheet is NOT a comprehensive disclosure document and you should read this policy's PDS (Product Disclosure Statement) before taking out the policy.
