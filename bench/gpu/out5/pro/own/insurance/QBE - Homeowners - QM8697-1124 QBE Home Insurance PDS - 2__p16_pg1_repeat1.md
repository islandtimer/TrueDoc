Sum(s) insured adjustment feature

We’ll increase your sum(s) insured by 0.5% for each whole calendar month until you next renew your policy to help with increases in replacement costs.

We will not increase your sum(s) insured for any Specified Contents or Portable Contents covered under your policy.

For an example of how the sum(s) insured adjustment feature works in practice, see our Home Insurance Additional Information Guide at qbe.com/au or call us on 133 723 for a copy at no charge by us.
