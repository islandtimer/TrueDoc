Buildings and/or Contents cover — what's the difference?

Here’s a brief outline of the difference between Buildings and Contents cover, to help you decide the cover you need.

<table><tr><td>Buildings cover</td><td>Contents cover</td></tr><tr><td>Designed to cover the physical structures that make up your home, like the house itself, garage, sheds, swimming pools and decks. Generally for customers who own a freehold home – like a house, townhouse, terrace or semi-detached dwelling. See pages 28 to 29 for a detailed list of what we define as buildings.</td><td>Designed to cover items at your home – from furniture, appliances and white goods to personal belongings like computers, clothing and shoes. It also covers your carpets and window furnishings. See pages 36 to 37 for a detailed list of what we define as contents.</td></tr></table>
