HEREDITY, HOW OPPOSED.

The doctrine of heredity, so ably argued in late numbers of THE ALPHA, deserves further notice at the hands of its advocates. I proceed to discuss the office.

The Hindooism might be compressed in one—the ready acceptance of the cheap, the superficial, the pretentious; or we may specify two principal forms of the same tendency. One is the baptismal regeneration, the other is the exalted into the thing signified, the sacramental water removes the stain and disabilities of inherited depravity.
