The above was a part of a letter written by M. D. Stone, N.Y., M. to a friend the morning after the news reached here of the capture of the Mass troops in the city of Baltimore 18th 1914, of April 28th.

I come now to the expression in your excellent letter about I have also along "fevered" are our ladies needed. I shall not wait and shall see "fevered" from the fact I have directed lest a sense of vague uncertainty in regard to matters here should discourage the efforts of our patriotic ladies at home—it was this fear and only this which can give one courage to assemble the worthy ladies of your committee (although my superior) to come up and assist us if the desire of the people demands it.
