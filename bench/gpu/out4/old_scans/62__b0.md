"Glen Echo, Md.,  
February 1, 1902.

Mr. Alfred T. Osmond,  
Trenton, N.J.

Dear Mr. Osmond:-

It would surely carry you back many years if I were to say "My dear pupil," still it would be true, and I want to return you my thanks at once for remembering that you were my pupil, and the dear old days of the school, that really meant so much, for they have lived until today, and never in any way has it passed out of the minds, I think, of any pupil I was permitted to have. Their life-long loyal allegiance to me is beyond my comprehension. Little, as many of them were, trifling as the days must have seemed,
