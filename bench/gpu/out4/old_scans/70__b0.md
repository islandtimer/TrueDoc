"The child has hardly come to knowledge a family of eight idiots, all grown to men's estate, with stalwart bodies, but not sufficient mental calibre to care for their persons, and yet they are in every other character idiots. Their father and mother are cousins. With the habits common to ignorant people, blood poisoned with whisky and tobacco, the tissues of the body built up on pork and oatmeal, and the mind so debased, they are discouraged in their poverty and the failure of all the objects of life? Would not legislation that would have interfered in preventing the marriage of such persons, or after their birth, have saved its burdensome existence, restraints to have prevented these eight crimes from burdening the world have been wise and timely?

"This is the class that are damned into the world, not by nature, but by the wanton indulgence of themselves and those who should have been their guardians."

It would make a vast difference in that man's conduct, and even more in the character of his children, if he should hold himself responsible in this matter of generation, and while holding the balance of power is largely responsible for the endowment of his children. "As he sow so shall he reap."

"God created the nation of all souls, but he cannot and will not save souls without human help.

"This knowledge will mark a new era in the history of human progress and reform. Men will select their wives for their vigorous health, their moral and spiritual tendencies by inheritance and the effect of personal habits of life, and the present respect to their own persons, or at least their own opinions, before they dare jeopardize the welfare of offspring, who are so helplessly dependent upon their ancestors not only for the breath of life, but for qualities which will shape their whole lives, and for qualities which will enable them to avoid the enormous burdens to themselves and society."

THE WOMEN'S NATIONAL RELIEF ASSOCIATION.

In the autumn of 1880, plans, long considered by a few benevolently-minded women, culminated in the formation of the Women's National Relief Association, having its headquarters in Washington, D.C., the object of which is so to organize benevolent effort that the women of the nation, by concert of action, may have available at any season an imperative necessity arising from conditions of other than epidemic, famine or war, such sanitary aid and material as the occasion calls for, and to insure their economical and efficient use. The immediate and most important specific object of supplying without delay the United States Life-Saving Stations with beds, blankets, warm clothing, and other necessaries for the preservation and comfort of the men and boys stationed there.
